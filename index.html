Privacy Policy

promote.fun Discord bot · Last updated 15 August 2026

This policy explains what the promote.fun Discord bot ("the bot") stores about you, why it stores it, who else can see it, and how to have it deleted.

The bot is private. It is not listed in any bot directory, has no public invite link, and runs only on Discord servers operated by the promote.fun team. It is not available for installation on servers we do not control.

If you use our Discord server, this policy applies to you. It covers the bot only. Discord's own handling of your data is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

1. What we store

We store the minimum needed to run a referral programme, review campaign applications, answer support requests and keep the server safe. Everything below is tied to your Discord user ID.

Account and membership

  • Your Discord user ID, username and display name
  • The date your Discord account was created
  • When you joined and, if applicable, left our server
  • Which roles you hold on our server

Why: to identify you across features, to credit referrals to the right person, and to gate staff-only commands. Account age is used to detect referral fraud.

Referral and invite records

  • Which server invite you used when you joined, and who created that invite
  • Referral totals per member, and the history of credits added or removed
  • Fraud review outcomes: whether a referral was accepted, rejected or flagged, and the staff decision behind it

Why: members earn rewards for referring genuine new members. We have to record who referred whom in order to pay correctly, and we have to be able to detect and reverse fraudulent referrals such as alt accounts and self-invites.

Message activity count

  • A running count of how many messages you have sent in the server

This is a number only. We do not store the content of your ordinary messages. Why: an account that joins and never speaks is a signal of a fake referral. The count helps staff tell real members from farmed accounts.

Moderation records

  • Warnings issued to you, including the reason and the staff member who issued it
  • Automated moderation events: when a message of yours was removed by the safety filter, which rule it matched, and a copy of the removed message so staff can review the decision

Why: to keep the server safe from scams, phishing and malware, and so that moderation decisions can be reviewed and reversed if wrong.

Support tickets

  • The contents of the private ticket channel you open, saved as a transcript when the ticket closes
  • Any files, screenshots or images you upload in a ticket
  • Timestamps used to measure how quickly staff responded and resolved the issue

Why: so staff can pick up where a colleague left off, so we can reference past issues, and so we can measure and improve our support response times.

Campaign applications and payouts

  • Social media handles and profile links you submit
  • Audience analytics screenshots you upload
  • Links to content you produced for a campaign
  • Your promote.fun username, if you link it
  • Payout amounts, status and approval decisions

Why: to review your application, verify the work, and pay you.

Staff only

If you are a member of staff, we additionally record shift sign-ins, sign-outs, breaks and activity timestamps, used for rota management.

2. What we do not store

  • The content of your ordinary messages. The bot reads messages in real time to run the safety filter, but only stores a message when that filter removes it.
  • The content of your direct messages. The bot does not read, log or act on the content of DMs you send it.
  • Your presence or online status. The bot does not request the Presence intent.
  • Voice channel audio. Never recorded or processed.
  • Payment details. Card and bank details are never handled by the bot. Payouts are processed on the promote.fun platform, outside Discord.
  • Anything at all about servers we do not operate.

3. Direct messages from the bot

The bot may DM you:

  • The outcome of a campaign application or payout you submitted
  • Forms and confirmations for a process you started yourself
  • Reminders relating to a ticket or shift you are already part of
  • Campaign announcements, if you opted in or hold a role you were given for that purpose

You can stop campaign announcement DMs at any time using the opt-out button on any such message, or by asking a staff member. Opting out is permanent until you reverse it. Transactional DMs about something you submitted are not marketing and cannot be opted out of separately; stop submitting, or ask us to remove your data.

6. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you. We will send you your records.
  • Correct anything wrong, for example a wrongly rejected referral.
  • Delete your data. We will remove your records, subject to the audit note below.
  • Stop DMing you. Handled immediately.

To make any of these requests, open a support ticket on our Discord server or email us at the address in section 8. Include your Discord username. We will respond within 30 days.

One limit on deletion: if you have been paid, or have a payout pending, we keep the minimum record of that transaction for the audit period in section 5. We will delete everything else. If you have been banned for fraud, we keep the ban record so the ban can be enforced.

If you are in the UK or EU, our lawful bases are contract (running the referral programme and paying you), legitimate interests (server safety, fraud prevention, support quality), and consent (campaign announcement DMs, which you may withdraw at any time). You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Our Discord server and the promote.fun platform are for users aged 13 and over, in line with Discord's Terms of Service, and 16 and over where local law sets a higher minimum age for Discord. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If we learn that we have, we delete it and remove the account from the server. If you believe a child is using our server, contact us and we will act.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the bot uses your data in any material way, we will update this page and announce the change in our Discord server before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this version was published.
